## Status Codes

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|

| Interview | Client invited you to interview |

## Monthly Goals

### Month 1
- **Target proposals**: 150-200
- **Target responses**: 15-30 (10-15%)
- **Target jobs**: 5-10
- **Target revenue**: $500-1500
- **Target reviews**: 5-10 five-star

### Month 2
- **Target proposals**: 150-200
- **Target responses**: 20-40 (15-20%)
- **Target jobs**: 8-12
- **Target revenue**: $1000-2500
- **Target reviews**: 8-12 five-star

### Month 3
- **Target proposals**: 120-180
- **Target responses**: 25-50 (20-25%)
- **Target jobs**: 10-15
- **Target revenue**: $2000-4000
- **Target reviews**: 10-15 five-star

## Tips for Tracking

1. **Update daily**: Fill in spreadsheet every evening
2. **Review weekly**: Analyze what's working
3. **Adjust strategy**: Based on response rates
4. **Track patterns**: Best times to apply, best job types
5. **Celebrate wins**: Every 5-star review matters

## Analysis Questions

### Weekly Review
1. Which job types got the most responses?
2. What time of day did you get responses?
3. Which proposal templates worked best?
4. What rates are clients accepting?
5. What common objections do you hear?

### Monthly Review
1. Are you hitting your targets?
2. Which services are most in demand?
3. Should you raise or lower rates?
4. Which clients should you build relationships with?
5. What new skills should you learn?
