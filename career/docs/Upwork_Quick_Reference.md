## Red Flags (Avoid These Jobs)

### Budget Red Flags
- ❌ "Budget: $1" (unrealistic)
- ❌ "I'll pay you after" (scam)
- ❌ No budget specified (waste of time)

### Deadline Red Flags
- ❌ "I need this in 1 hour" (unreasonable)
- ❌ "Due yesterday" (impossible)

### Communication Red Flags
- ❌ "This is confidential" (no details)
- ❌ Vague job descriptions
- ❌ No payment verification

### Client Red Flags
- ❌ No profile picture
- ❌ No job history
- ❌ Bad reviews from other freelancers
- ❌ "I've been scammed before" (red flag)
