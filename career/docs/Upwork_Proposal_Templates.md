## Usage Notes

1. **Always personalize** — Replace [Client Name] and [specific task] with actual details
2. **Match the template** to the job posting's main service
3. **Lead with value** — What you can do for them
4. **Include social proof** — Years of experience, number of projects, etc.
5. **Offer a sample** — Free sample builds trust quickly
6. **Be concise** — Clients scan, not read
